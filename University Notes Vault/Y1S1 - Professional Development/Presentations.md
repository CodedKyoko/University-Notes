## What makes a good Presentation?

### Structure

- Tell them what your going to say (Introduction)
	- Introduce the topic
	- Explain the purpose of the presentation
	- Outline what will be covered

- Say it (middle)
	- Explain the main ideas
	- Explain the technical concepts
	- Present evidence/ examples

- Tell them what you said (Conclusion)
	- Summarise key points
	- Reinforce the main message
	- Provide recommendations/conclusions

### Presenting as a Group

- Allocate sections of the presentation fairly
- Use consistent terminology and slide design
- **practice transitions between speakers**
- Ensure everyone understands the complete presentation
- Rehearse Together

### Rule of 3

- A title (what is the slide about)
- Some text (The information)
- A graphic/ image (Illustrates the information)
	- Provides a balance to the information
	- {something} isolated white background (or PNG) in google

>[!Warning]
> Avoid overly complicated backgrounds
> Avoid Text heavy slides
> 5/5/5
> 5 words per line
> 5 bullet points per slide
> 5 slides at a time

Practice.
