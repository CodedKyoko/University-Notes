IDEAL is a problem solving framework for tackling unfamiliar problems

## Identify (The Problem)

Outline the known facts, constraints and unknowns.

## Describe (The Outcome)

What do you want to be the result of this process?

## Explore (Possible Strategies)

Brainstorm possible approaches to the problem.

## Anticipate (Outcomes and Act)

Which is the best approach, pros and cons?

## Look (and Learn)

Reflect on the process, what has gone well/ not so well.
