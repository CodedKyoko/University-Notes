>[!Note]
>Problem solving is *iterative* - it happens in cycles

### Plan

Identify the Problem and **plan** for change

>[!Example]
>"The WiFi in my room keeps disconnecting"
>
>PLAN: Eliminate disconnections by moving nearer to access point.

### Do

Implement (**do**) the plan

>[!Example]
>Physically move the laptop closer to the access point.

### Check

Review (**check**) the results

>[!Example]
>Monitor the connection for 24 hours.

### Act

Take **act**ion based on findings (go back to planning stage)

>[!Example]
>IF improved, standardise the solution, IF NOT return to planning phase.

>[!Note]
>"ACT" does not mean finish; it means decide what to do NEXT.
