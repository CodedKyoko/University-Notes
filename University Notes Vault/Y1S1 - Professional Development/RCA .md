RCA is short for Root Cause Analysis.

A systematic problem-solving process used to identify and fix the underlying origin of a problem – rather than just fixing the surface-level symptoms

# Visualising Problems

## Flowcharts

Flowcharts allow us to visually represent the logical flow in a program/ system/ algorithm.

They

- Encourage us to break problems into steps
  
- Identify decisions and choices that must be made

- Make it easier to identify problems visually

## Psuedocode

Psuedocode is a language agnostic non functional non compile-able non interpret-able simplified coding language with no hard syntax, used for quickly mocking up ideas.

# The 5 Whys

>[!Example]
>Problem: The new software update was launched two weeks later than planned.  
>
>Why 1: Why was the software update released late?  
>>“The testing phase took twice as long as expected.”  
>
>Why 2: Why did the testing phase take twice as long as planned?  
>>“The testing team found a high number of critical bugs just before release.  
>
>Why 3: Why did they find so many critical bugs just before release?  
>>“The developers were working from outdated requirements during programming.  
>
>Why 4: Why were the developers working from outdated requirements?  
>>“The product manager didn’t notify the developers that the requirements had changed .  
>
>Why 5 (Root Cause): Why didn’t the product manager notify the developers?  
>>“There is no automated system for alerting team members when requirements change.
