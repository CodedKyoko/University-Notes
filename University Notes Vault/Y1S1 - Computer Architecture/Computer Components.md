Transisters: A tiny "switch", made of silicon - which is a semiconductor.

Integrated Circuit: aka a "micro chip"; built from 10 to 10 billion transistors.

PSU: Provides power

Proccesor/ CPU:

MainBoard/ Motherboard: PCB; CMOS battery, CPU Socket, I/O (Ports (USB, Ethernet,...) Wireless (Wifi, Bluetooth)), RAM Slots and PCIE.
	NorthBridge handles "Fast I/O" (Ie PCIe ports)
	Southbride handles "Slow I/O" (Ie Ports, ethernet etc)

RAM: Primary Storage/ Memory; Volatile; Stores ephemeral data for CPU to use.

Hard Drive/ SSD: Secondary storage, used to store file.

Systems on a Chip: All Chip based systems on a single peice of silicon.

CPU, Storage and IO connected by BUSSES.


CPU made of ALU, registers and CU; also connected by BUSSES.
